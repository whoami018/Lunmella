Lunmella is an open-source Android application for downloading public videos, reels, and audio from Facebook and Instagram.

Features:
- Paste links from your clipboard or share links directly to Lunmella from other apps
- Inspect available video resolutions (Auto HD, 1080p, 720p, 480p, and source formats)
- Extract audio-only tracks in M4A format
- Automatic video and audio stream merging via FFmpeg
- Real-time download progress, ETA display, and one-tap cancellation
- Saves media directly to your device Downloads folder and opens files with your preferred player
- In-app Settings option to update the bundled yt-dlp extraction engine

Notes:
- Only works with publicly accessible links (does not bypass login or private content restrictions)
- Powered by yt-dlp, FFmpeg, and youtubedl-android
- Free and open-source software (GPL-3.0-only) with no ads or tracking
